## Wear

An accepted installation starts to wear. Faults do not fall from the sky: each one is a threshold on a quantity the model already tracks. The maintenance panel gathers them and shows their **severity**:

- NORMAL;
- ATTENTION: small effect, plan the repair;
- NEEDS REPAIR: noticeable loss of efficiency, protections trip more often;
- CRITICAL: failure is near.

Six lamps on the face of the panel show the colour of the worst fault in their category. Losses grow slowly up to half wear and steeply towards the end.

## Faults

- **Bearing.** The steam engine, fan, pump and hoist run on plain bearings with bronze shells. Their life is about 12 hours of play when greased with tallow. At the end the shell seizes and the machine stops. Repair: a [bronze bearing shell](item:promptcritical:bronze_bearing_shell), machine stopped, 60 s. Shells are cast in your own foundry: one bronze bearing housing casting gives two.
- **Lubrication.** An [oil can](item:promptcritical:oil_can) with [tallow](item:promptcritical:tallow) in your inventory, allowed while running. Without lubrication the bearing wears many times faster. Tallow comes from mixing rotten flesh in Create.
- **Scale** in the fire-tube boiler: a [scraper](item:promptcritical:boiler_scraper) on a boiler that is stopped, cooled and drained. The boiler drain is opened on its panel.
- **Gland** on the engine rod or pump shaft steams or leaks. Tighten it with a [wrench](item:promptcritical:torque_wrench) while running, up to three times; then replace the [packing](item:promptcritical:gland_packing) with the machine stopped.
- **Impeller** of the drainage pump is eaten by acid mine water three times faster than by neutral water. Head falls below the passport. Replace with a [pump impeller](item:promptcritical:pump_impeller), stopped, 120 s.
- **Rope** of the hoist: replaced with [wire rope](item:promptcritical:wire_rope).

Repairs are done on a stopped machine. You can reach into a running one, but its moving parts will hit you, and the log will record it.

## Finding bearing wear

The same fault shows earlier to whoever has the better instrument:

- **By ear.** [Mechanic's stethoscope](item:promptcritical:mechanic_stethoscope), right-click a running machine: "steady hum" is normal, "rustle" is stage one, "grinding" stage two, "knocking" stage three and beyond. Hissing is the gland.
- **By hand.** Shift + right-click with an empty hand on the housing: "warm", "hot", "scalding". Heat shows from stage three. "Scalding" costs health.

From the first rustle to failure runs about 40 % of the life, several hours of play. That is enough to notice and to plan a stop. A stethoscope round once a shift is cheaper than a new shell and the downtime.

## The recording gauge

A [recording gauge](item:promptcritical:recording_gauge) goes on an installation. On a boiler it records pressure, on an engine or fan speed, on a pump flow. The drum turns once an hour of real time, one point every 5 seconds. Load it with [chart paper](item:promptcritical:chart_paper), right-click. With an empty hand the paper comes off as a [recorded chart](item:promptcritical:recorded_chart); right-click with it in hand to read it.

The recording gauge is incident review without a computer. A fusible plug has gone: take off the chart and see when the pressure went wrong and what the operator did in the minute before.
