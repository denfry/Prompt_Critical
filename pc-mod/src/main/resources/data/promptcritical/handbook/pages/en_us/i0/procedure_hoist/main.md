## Purpose

This procedure sets out how to prepare, start and run a mine hoisting installation: a drum hoist on a Create shaft, a headframe with a sheave, a cage in the shaft and a wire rope.

## Before start-up

- The headframe is built from 2–4 segments and stands in front of or behind the hoist, no more than 32 blocks away.
- The shaft under the headframe is clear to full depth. The cage stands in the shaft at the top landing.
- At every level there is a way out beside the cage: two free blocks over a solid floor.
- A Create shaft is connected to the drum coupling. Rated speed is 64 rpm. The network must have spare power to lift the load.
- The rope is hung: a stack of coils right-clicked onto the hoist. There are enough coils for the shaft depth plus the run over the sheave.
- The panel opens with an empty hand: landings are listed, protections are reset, the speed limit is set.

## Starting a trip

- Load the cage. A person is 80 kg, a minecart 300 kg.
- Close the cage door with an empty hand. With the door open the hoist will not start.
- Choose a landing on the panel.
- The hoist checks the rope safety factor. Limits: man-riding 9, mixed duty 7.5, materials 6.5. Below the limit, it refuses to start.
- If refused, reduce the load. Do not carry men and materials together if the margin does not cover mixed duty.

## Running

- Set the speed limit for the duty: men travel slower than coal. The maximum is 12 m/s.
- Avoid overspeed: more than 15 % over the limit trips the protection.
- Watch rope wear. When the rope is reported due for replacement, change it at the first stop.
- Change the rope only with the cage stopped: right-click the hoist with a stack of new coils.

## Shutdown

- Wait until the cage stands at a landing and is disassembled.
- The "Stop" button halts the cage in the shaft. Use it whenever anything is unclear.

## Emergencies

- **Protection tripped: overspeed, overwind or loss of power.** The hoist is on the brake. Find the cause: Create network overload, a broken shaft, a wrong speed limit. Clear it, then press "Reset trip".
- **Rope broken, cage on its safety catches.** Bring people out through the nearest level. Replace the rope and raise the cage.
- **Rope broken, cage fell.** Do not run the hoist until the shaft has been inspected and the rope and cage replaced.
- **Shaft flooding.** Check the drainage. A flooded part of the shaft is not a landing.
- Never ride men on a rope that has already been reported worn.
