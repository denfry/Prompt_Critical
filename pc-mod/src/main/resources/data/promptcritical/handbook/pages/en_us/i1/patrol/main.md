# Equipment rounds

Telemetry sees what its sensors see. A man walking the plant hears a bearing before the vibration transmitter does and sees a tag on a valve that the screen does not show.

Fix a [patrol checkpoint](item:promptcritical:patrol_checkpoint) on each item of equipment of the round. The route lives in the [patrol reader](item:promptcritical:patrol_reader): sneak and right-click the checkpoints in the order of the round to add them. On the round, right-click each checkpoint in turn: the reader stamps the visit and gives you the card of the point — running machines within 4 blocks, a worn bearing heard by ear, hum from imbalance or misalignment, locked isolation points, open work permits, a confined space with bad air behind its manway. A checkpoint taken out of order does not count; go back for the one you missed.

The round must be walked within an hour of real time. A checkpoint that has not been stamped for longer lights its lamp, and the plant journal records the overdue round. Sneak and right-click in the air with the reader to see how much time is left.
