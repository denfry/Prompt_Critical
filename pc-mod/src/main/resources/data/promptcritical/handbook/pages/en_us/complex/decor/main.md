A plant is built to last, and it should look like a plant. The first-epoch decor is the finish of industrial buildings: workshops, boiler houses, corridors and control rooms.

## Walls and floors

- **Ceramic tiles** in white, blue and green: change rooms, laboratories, washrooms.
- **Porcelain tiles** in grey and beige: engine-hall floors.
- **Painted concrete** in grey, yellow and fire red. Yellow marks hazardous edges and columns; red marks fire-fighting equipment.
- **Two-tone walls**: green, blue or beige oil paint below, white above. Industrial corridors were painted this way for decades.
- **Glass blocks**, clear and frosted: they let light through but not the view.
- **Corrugated steel** in grey, white, blue, green and red: warehouses and light workshops.
- **Curb**: for roads and yards between plants.

Tiles, porcelain tiles and painted concrete come with slabs, stairs and walls. Cut them from full blocks on a stonecutter.

## Platforms and guarding

- **Steel grating** and grating steps: service platforms over equipment. You can see what is below.
- **Handrails**, yellow and galvanised.
- **Caged ladder**: a vertical ladder with hoops, used to climb boilers, chimneys and headframes.

## Lights, signs, clocks

- **Lights**: industrial, street bracket, floodlight and a green "Exit" sign. A light is placed switched on; right-click with an empty hand toggles it. In a gassy mine a light is safer than a torch, because a torch is an open flame.
- **Room signs** and **evacuation arrows**.
- **Safety posters**, small and large.
- **Wall clock**: shows game time. Handy in a control room to see when night is coming.

## Document lectern

A [document lectern](item:promptcritical:document_lectern) holds one document: an operating procedure or the handbook.

- Right-click with a document in hand to place it.
- Right-click with an empty hand to open and read it. Anyone can read it.
- Sneak and right-click with an empty hand to take the document back.

A comparator next to the lectern gives a signal while a document is on it. Put a lectern with the boiler procedure by the boiler and one with the hoist procedure by the hoist. A newcomer on the server will read the procedure before turning anything.
