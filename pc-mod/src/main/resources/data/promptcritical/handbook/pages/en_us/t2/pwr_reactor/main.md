## The pressurized water reactor

The T2a starter unit makes 100 MW of heat in a vessel of 19 assemblies. Primary water is both moderator and coolant, and it must not boil: 15.7 MPa keeps it liquid at 320 °C.

The plant is 3×6×3: the vessel with the core below, the rod drive head above. The control desk is on the controller. Within 12 blocks you need:

- a **pressurizer** — without one the protection will not let you cock the scram rods;
- two **reactor coolant pumps** on Create shafts, loops 1 and 2. The shaft only holds the speed. Cut the drive and the rotor with its flywheel coasts down by itself, halving the flow in 12 seconds;
- **steam generators** against water-tube boiler drums. The steam goes to the turbines you already have.

Fuel is VVER-440, VVER-1000 or PWR 17×17 assemblies from fabrication, 3–5 %. One design per vessel. Put them in the desk slot or feed them by hopper: on a shut-down reactor they go in from the centre. Nineteen VVER-440 assemblies hold about 2.3 t of uranium.

Unloading takes one assembly at a time, the most burnt first.
