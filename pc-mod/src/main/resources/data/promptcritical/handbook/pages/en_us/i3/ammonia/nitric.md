## Nitric acid

The [nitric acid unit](item:promptcritical:nitric_acid_unit) follows Ostwald. An air compressor on the Create shaft mixes air with ammonia, and the mixture passes a pack of platinum-rhodium gauze at 800–900 °C: 4NH₃ + 5O₂ → 4NO + 6H₂O. Some ammonia burns to nitrogen instead, and the NO yield is 94–98 %. It is higher at atmospheric pressure and lower at 0.73 MPa, but there the equipment is smaller and the acid stronger. The scheme (atmospheric, 0.42 MPa or 0.73 MPa like the Soviet UKL-7) is switched with the unit stopped.

The gauze goes in by hand. Platinum slowly leaves with the gas, faster at higher pressure and hotter gauze. Change the pack when less than half of the platinum is left. Keep ammonia at 10–11 % of the air. Above the lower explosive limit (about 15 % at 20 °C, less with preheat and pressure; the panel shows it) the mixture flashes inside the converter and tears the gauze.

The nitrous gas is cooled and NO is oxidised by oxygen to NO₂. This is one of the few reactions that run faster in the cold, and pressure speeds it up a lot. In the tray column NO₂ is absorbed by water: 3NO₂ + H₂O → 2HNO₃ + NO, and the released NO is oxidised again. The stronger the acid on a tray, the worse it absorbs. The limit is about 50 % at atmospheric pressure, 58 % at 0.42 MPa and 62 % at 0.73 MPa. Set the strength on the panel and the unit meters the water. Ask for more than the limit and the bottom trays stop working while NOx in the tail gas climbs. Above 1500 ppm you can see it: a brown "fox tail" hangs over the unit. The tail gas catalyst reduces NOx with ammonia back to nitrogen.

The acid is delivered as 56 %, with all of its HNO₃ counted.

## Concentration

Water and nitric acid form an azeotrope at 68.4 %, and plain distillation will not go stronger. The [acid concentrator](item:promptcritical:acid_concentrator) adds magnesium nitrate melt, which binds the water, and 97–99 % acid comes off the top. The diluted melt is evaporated over a fire burning sales gas. Set the melt ratio and the reflux ratio on the panel. If the top is weaker than 97 %, the column runs on itself and delivers nothing.
