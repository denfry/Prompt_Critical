The world has weather you cannot see in the sky: air temperature, wind, sunshine and the season. A year is four seasons of 24 game days each (configurable); summer is about 20 °C warmer than winter, day about 10 °C warmer than night. The biome sets the climate: about 15 °C mean annual on plains, hotter in a desert, colder on tundra. A server operator can check the weather with `/pc env`.

## Wind

Wind changes on three scales. Slowly, over a few days, weather systems pass; over a day the wind rises by day and drops at night; on top come gusts lasting seconds to minutes. Gustiness follows the wind energy standard IEC 61400-1. Rain strengthens the wind, a thunderstorm doubles it, winter is windier. The higher above ground, the stronger the wind, especially over water and open fields; forest slows it down.

## What depends on it

- **Overhead lines** cool better the stronger the wind (convection per IEEE 738), and the sun heats them. On a hot calm day a line carries less current than on a windy winter night.
- **The mine.** The rock keeps the mean climate temperature all year and gets warmer with depth. In winter cold outside air is heavier and sinks down the shaft by itself; in summer the natural draft weakens and can reverse. The main fan has to cope in every season.
