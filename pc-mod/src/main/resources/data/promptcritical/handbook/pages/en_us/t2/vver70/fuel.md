## Spent fuel pool and refuelling machine

The **pool** is 5 × 5 m and 8 m deep, with 256 rack positions. Four metres of water over the cassette heads shield the hall. The cooling and make-up pumps draw 30 kW from the site board. Without power the decay heat warms the water until it boils off. If the level falls below the cooling suction (6.5 m), the pumps stand idle until the water is made up. Uncovered cassettes heat up; at 800 °C their cladding bursts and fuel aerosol escapes into the hall.

Right-click the pool for a summary: level, temperature, decay heat, water over the heads and the dose rate at the surface. A fresh cassette can be put in by hand and taken back with Shift + right-click. An irradiated one cannot be reached by hand.

The **refuelling machine** is a bridge on rails over the reactor shaft and the pool. Both the vessel and the pool must lie under its runway.

- Right-click unloads: the machine takes the most burnt cassette and sets it in a pool rack.
- Shift + right-click loads: the machine carries a fresh cassette from the pool into the core.

Only a shut-down reactor can be refuelled. The machine runs on the site board. The bridge and trolley travel 4 s per metre, the mast 5 s per metre, and the grip takes 3 s. While the machine is busy, right-click shows what it is doing.
