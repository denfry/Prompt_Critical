## The boiling water reactor

The same vessel of 19 assemblies, but the water boils right in the core at 7 MPa and the steam goes from the vessel to the turbine. No steam generator, no pressurizer: the turbine regulator holds the pressure and the feedwater regulator holds the level.

The plant is 3×6×3: the vessel with the core below, steam separators and dryers above. Within 12 blocks of the desk you need:

- two **recirculation pumps** on Create shafts — the same reactor coolant pumps, flywheel coastdown included;
- a **water-tube boiler drum** with a steam generator next to it. Here it is the vessel's steam space: the steam reaches the turbines through it. No drum — the steam has nowhere to go and the pressure climbs to the safety valve.

There is no boron. Rods hold the excess reactivity: scram rods and control rods. The hydraulic scram drives them in from below in 3 s.

The steam carries **nitrogen-16**, made from the oxygen of the water under neutrons. Its half-life is 7 s and its gamma is 6 MeV. While the reactor is at power, steam turbines within 24 blocks of the desk shine. Shut it down and a minute later the nitrogen is gone.

VVER-1000 assemblies work best: a boiling core needs volume. In a small VVER-440 core the steam raises neutron leakage so much that it will not hold power.
