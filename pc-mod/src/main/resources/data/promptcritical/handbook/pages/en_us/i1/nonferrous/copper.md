## Copper

Copper ore is poor: Norilsk-type chalcopyrite carries about 3 % Cu, porphyry ore under 1 %. The metal has to be concentrated, smelted, blown and refined.

The [flotation cell](item:promptcritical:flotation_cell) takes raw chalcopyrite or pentlandite by hand or hopper; the grade comes from the assay. Pipe in water and coal tar: the cresols in the tar are the frother, 50 g per tonne of ore. Without tar there is no froth and nothing floats. The cell recovers 88 % of the metal into concentrate with 25 % Cu (chalcopyrite) or 10 % Ni (pentlandite, marked as the copper-nickel line). The tailings go to waste.

The [reverberatory smelter](item:promptcritical:matte_smelter) melts concentrate with vein quartz on fuel gas and oxygen. Part of the iron sulphide burns: iron goes into the slag with the silica, sulphur leaves as SO₂, and copper stays in matte with 55 % Cu. The same furnace smelts cassiterite concentrate with coke into tin.

The [Peirce–Smith converter](item:promptcritical:peirce_smith_converter) blows air through the matte. In the slag blow, 2FeS + 3O₂ → 2FeO + 2SO₂, and the iron oxide needs quartz to make slag; without quartz the blow waits. In the copper blow, Cu₂S + O₂ → 2Cu + SO₂, leaving blister copper at 99 %. With natural gas piped in and Refine switched on, the same vessel fire-refines the blister: oxidation, then poling with gas, giving anode copper at 99.5 %.

From concentrate to blister, every tonne of copper gives off about 2 t of SO₂. Put a [sulphuric acid plant](item:promptcritical:acid_plant) within 8 blocks of the smelter and converter, and the gas goes into its contact converter instead of pyrite. The acid goes on to uranium leaching. Without the acid plant the SO₂ goes up the stack into the emission ledger and onto the surrounding chunks.

The [electrorefining cell section](item:promptcritical:electrorefining_cell) is twenty cells in series on an electrolysis rectifier. Copper anodes dissolve in CuSO₄ and H₂SO₄ at 55–65 °C and plate out as 99.99 % cathodes at 250–350 A/m² and 0.25–0.35 V. At 0.3 V and 95 % current efficiency that is 0.27 kWh per kilogram. Gold, silver and platinum metals fall to the bottom as anode slime. A copper cathode crafts into nine copper ingots.
