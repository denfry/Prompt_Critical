## Tin

Cassiterite is heavy, and the light gangue washes off it. Pass raw cassiterite through a Create fan into water (bulk washing) to get cassiterite concentrate: 10 kg bags with 70 % tin. Smelt the concentrate with coke in the [reverberatory smelter](item:promptcritical:matte_smelter): SnO₂ + 2C → Sn + 2CO, on fuel gas. Tin comes out in 10 kg ingots.
