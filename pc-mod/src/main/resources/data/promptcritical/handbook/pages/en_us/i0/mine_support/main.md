The rock above a heading, the roof, holds itself up as long as the unsupported span is not too wide. Every block you take out widens the span. Exceed the permitted span and the roof starts to separate, and after a while it falls in a dome.

## The span rule

A roof block counts as supported if there is support within the permitted span: a pillar, a wall or a set of timber. The permitted span depends on the rock immediately above the heading.

- **Strong**: sandstone, granite, diorite, andesite, deepslate: 6 blocks. Once exceeded, it holds for about half an hour of physical time, tens of seconds in game.
- **Medium**: other stone, mudstone in effect: 4 blocks. A few minutes of physical time, seconds in game.
- **Weak**: coal, fireclay, gravel, dirt, clay, sand: 2 blocks. It comes down almost at once.

The height of the fall dome grows with the excess: the wider the span beyond the limit, the higher the cavity. A roof fall is not only dangerous in itself; it blocks the heading and changes the ventilation.

A roof of coal itself is weak. If you drive along the seam with coal overhead, support closely.

## Timber support

A [mine support](item:promptcritical:mine_support) is a timber set 3 blocks high. It goes under the roof, adds 2 blocks to the permitted span and carries about 50 kN.

- Set the support before the span is exceeded, not after. Support first, then the next block.
- Timber rots in a wet heading, about one per cent a day. Old timber holds less.
- An explosion breaks supports. A damaged set carries less than half of what it did; replace it.

## Pillars

The most reliable support is solid ground left in place. Between parallel headings, leave pillars of coal or rock. Robbing a pillar for an extra hundred kilograms of coal is a common and expensive mistake.

## Further on

Longwall faces use [powered roof supports](promptcritical:i0/longwall): they hold the roof over the shearer and advance behind it. Behind the last support the roof is deliberately allowed to cave; that is what a goaf looks like. The steel epoch brings yielding steel arches with a wider span.
