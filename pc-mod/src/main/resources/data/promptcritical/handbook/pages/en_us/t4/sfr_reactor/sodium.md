## Sodium

The primary loop runs at almost atmospheric pressure, 0.1 MPa, from 380 to 550 °C: sodium boils only at 883 °C. Nothing pushes the coolant out of a break, but sodium burns in air.

- **Sodium fire.** If the core outlet goes above 700 °C, the primary piping creeps and leaks. Sodium burns to Na₂O at about 9 MJ per kg and fills the hall with thick white smoke. The fire spreads around the reactor.
- **Sodium–water reaction.** If the intermediate loop goes above 650 °C, the steam generator tubes crack and water reaches the sodium: Na + H₂O → NaOH + ½H₂, with heat and hydrogen. The hydrogen detector scrams the reactor; once the gas reaches 1 MPa, the rupture disc bursts.

That is why there is an intermediate loop at all: radioactive primary sodium never meets water.
