Pig iron is brittle: it holds 4–5 % carbon, plus silicon, manganese and phosphorus. Steel is made by burning the excess out with oxygen. In a **basic oxygen converter** a jet of pure O₂ strikes a bath of molten iron from above; carbon leaves as carbon monoxide, silicon, manganese and phosphorus go into the slag. The heat of oxidation is enough to melt scrap as well: the converter needs no fuel.

## Oxygen: the air separation unit

Oxygen comes from the [air separation unit](item:promptcritical:air_separator). The cold box counts as assembled when the main compressor on a Create shaft, the molecular sieve unit and the expansion turbine stand within 8 blocks of it. The compressor needs cooling water in its tank. Output follows compressor power, about 0.42 kWh per cubic metre of O₂. Tanks sit around the cold box: nitrogen on the left, oxygen on the right, argon behind. Part of the power can go to liquid product, which flows into a [cryogenic tank](item:promptcritical:cryogenic_tank) nearby.

Start-up is slow: the cold box has to cool down first. Leave both protections on the panel switched on:

- **adsorber regeneration.** Without it the sieves pass moisture and CO₂, ice blocks the heat exchanger, and the unit stops to thaw;
- **hydrocarbon trip.** Air near a coke plant carries acetylene. It builds up in the liquid oxygen of the condenser. The trip stops the unit; drain the LOX. Without the trip the condenser explodes. A wrecked cold box is repaired with eight steel plates.

Keep the unit more than 24 blocks from the coke battery.

## The converter

The [converter](item:promptcritical:bof_vessel), 3×2×3, hangs on trunnions. A Create shaft through the trunnion port tilts it, about 4° a second at 64 RPM.

- **Charging**: tilt forward, toward the front, 40° or more. Hot metal pours from a ladle in front of the mouth, up to 12 t. [Scrap](item:promptcritical:steel_scrap) or cold pig iron and [burnt lime](item:promptcritical:burnt_lime) come from the panel slots.
- **Blowing**: upright only, with the [oxygen lance](item:promptcritical:oxygen_lance) lowered. The lance hangs over the mouth and takes oxygen by pipe. While the lance is in the mouth, the tilt drive is locked.
- **Tapping**: tilt back 80° or more, and steel runs into the ladle behind.

The panel sets oxygen flow and lance height. A 10 t heat blows in 14–15 seconds. A low lance over high-silicon iron makes the slag slop over. Overblowing oxidises iron and lowers the yield. Converter gas goes to the off-gas tank; it is a fuel.

Heat balance: 8 t of hot metal, 2 t of scrap and 0.5 t of lime give steel at 1600–1700 °C. More scrap runs colder. Lime makes the slag basic; without it the phosphorus stays in the metal. Lime is burnt from limestone or calcite in an ordinary Minecraft blast furnace.

## Ladle treatment and teeming

Steel straight from the blow is full of oxygen. At the [ladle treatment station](item:promptcritical:ladle_station) beside the ladle it is killed and alloyed: each button adds one [ferroalloy](item:promptcritical:ferroalloy), 100 kg, from its bin. Ferromanganese and ferrosilicon are crafted. Argon through the ladle plug stirs the bath and cools it a little. The panel shows the chemistry and the grade that will result.

The [ingot mold](item:promptcritical:ingot_mold) takes steel from a ladle set above it, up to a tonne. Once solid it is stripped as hot [ingots](item:promptcritical:steel_ingot) of 100 kg; the top with its shrinkage pipe goes to scrap. A refractory brick on an empty mold is a hot top: 12 % crop instead of 17 %. Steel that was not killed gives a porous ingot, and a porous ingot gives bad pipe.

An ingot cools on its own. Above 100 °C it burns in the inventory, above 500 °C it sets you alight. Once cold it stacks with cold ingots.

## Melt and water

The lance is water-cooled. A burnt-through lance pours water straight into the bath, and water under molten metal flashes to steam at once: a steam explosion. Keep the lance at least 0.2 m above the bath and raise it ("Park") as soon as the blow is over.
