# Entry, attendant and rescue

Entry is by a confined space work permit on the manway: write a blank permit on it, isolate, test the gas through the open hatch (stand within two blocks of it, or inside), admit. Sneak and right-click the manway to climb in or out. With strict permits the manway lets you in only under an active permit and with an attendant: another player outside, within 4 m, who does not go in. In advisory mode you get in anyway, with a warning in the plant journal.

Go in wearing a [safety harness](item:promptcritical:safety_harness) and a [lifeline](item:promptcritical:lifeline) clipped to a [rescue tripod](item:promptcritical:rescue_tripod) over the manway. If you fall unconscious, the attendant right-clicks the tripod and the winch pulls you out through the manway in five seconds, without anyone going in.

Going in after him without an [SCBA](item:promptcritical:scba) means the same air and the same faint. Most of those who die in confined spaces are the rescuers.
