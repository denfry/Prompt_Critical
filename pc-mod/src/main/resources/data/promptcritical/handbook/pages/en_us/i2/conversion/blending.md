# Gasoline blending and the laboratory

No unit makes motor gasoline by itself. It is blended from components, each with its own octane, vapour pressure, sulphur, benzene, aromatics, olefins and density: straight-run gasoline from the crude column (RON 68), reformate (98), cracked gasoline (91.5) and n-butane from the gas fractionation (93, but a vapour pressure of 360 kPa). The [blending station](item:promptcritical:gasoline_blender) has a control valve for each, in m³/h, and mixes them into its tank; motor gasoline leaves from the bottom.

Finding the recipe is a linear programme: maximise the margin subject to RON, MON, vapour pressure (Chevron index RVP^1.25), sulphur, benzene ≤ 1 %, aromatics ≤ 35 %, olefins ≤ 18 % and density. Normal-80 can be made from these four (for example 2.09 m³/h straight-run, 0.64 reformate, 5.0 cracked gasoline); AI-92 cannot, because MON 83 needs octane without aromatics, and that is alkylate, which is not built yet. A computer next to the station sees it as a pc_tank: getBlendData() returns the components, the grade, the analyzer and the passport, and setValves(a, b, c, d) sets the recipe.

The real blend misses the linear model a little: octane numbers do not add exactly by volume. A recipe on the MON limit drifts half a point off grade and has to be corrected by measurement.

Quality is invisible until it is measured. A [quality analyzer](item:promptcritical:quality_analyzer) touching the station shows RON, MON and RVP of the tank all the time, but it drifts: about 0.3 octane per hour of service. A [sample bottle](item:promptcritical:sample_bottle) used on the station, or on any tank with a gasoline component, takes a sample. The [quality lab](item:promptcritical:quality_lab) runs it on its CFR engines for 40 minutes of physical time and reports the result to ASTM reproducibility (±0.7 octane, ±5 % sulphur). A sample of the station's batch also writes the batch passport into the station and gives you a [batch certificate](item:promptcritical:batch_certificate). With a fresh passport the Calibrate button zeroes the analyzer drift. Blending more into the tank voids the passport.
