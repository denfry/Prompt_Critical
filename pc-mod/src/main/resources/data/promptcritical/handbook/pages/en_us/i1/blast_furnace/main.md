A blast furnace is a shaft furnace that never stops. Ore, coke and flux go in at the top in layers, the charges. Hot air is blown in at the bottom through the tuyeres. Coke at the tuyeres burns to carbon monoxide; the gas rises through the burden and strips the oxygen from the ore. Down in the hearth the iron picks up carbon and melts into hot metal, and the gangue with the flux becomes slag.

## Construction

The furnace is built freely. The shell is a 5×5 ring of [shell](item:promptcritical:blast_furnace_shell) blocks 12–18 high, with an empty 3×3 shaft inside. The bottom layer, the hearth floor, is a solid 5×5. The first layer above it holds exactly one [taphole](item:promptcritical:blast_furnace_taphole), the second holds 4–12 [tuyeres](item:promptcritical:blast_furnace_tuyere); neither may sit in a corner of the ring. The [furnace top](item:promptcritical:blast_furnace_top) sits over the middle. The [control panel](item:promptcritical:blast_furnace_controller) touches the ring from outside. Useful volume is the empty blocks of the shaft, 1 m³ each.

Auxiliaries are found by distance, within 12 blocks of the furnace:

- [blast blowers](item:promptcritical:blast_blower), 2×2×2 on a Create shaft: cold blast, more of it the faster the shaft turns;
- [hot blast stoves](item:promptcritical:hot_blast_stove), 3×9×3 Cowper stoves: "on gas" they burn fuel gas and heat their checkers, "on blast" they give that heat to the blast. The panel's auto cycle keeps one stove on blast and switches to the hottest;
- a [dust catcher](item:promptcritical:dust_catcher): top gas goes into its tank as clean blast-furnace gas. Without one the gas burns at the bleeders.

## Burden

Iron comes from [sinter](item:promptcritical:sinter) or lump ore. Magnetite is upgraded in the magnetic separator to concentrate, and the concentrate is sintered on the sinter strand with coke breeze and limestone. Hematite does not answer to a magnet; it is sintered as it is.

The burden goes into the furnace top by a Create funnel or from the storage of a skip contraption above it. Charges are made up by the **ore load** on the panel: kilograms of ore per kilogram of coke. A higher load means less coke per tonne of iron and a colder hearth. Flux, limestone or calcite, is set per unit of ore; it makes the slag fluid and basic, and basic slag takes up sulfur.

## Blast and heat

The blast sets the pace: every cubic metre of air burns its share of coke at the tuyeres. Hot blast (900 °C by default) brings heat in and saves coke. The panel shows the raceway temperature. Above 2200 °C the burden starts to melt up in the shaft; much lower and the hearth goes cold. A cold hearth and acid slag give high-sulfur iron.

Hanging risk grows with a raceway that is too hot, with driving the furnace beyond 2.5 t per cubic metre a day, and with weak coke. A hung burden stands still and then drops: a **slip**, with a gas surge through the bleeders and a chilled hearth.

## Modes

- **Cold → blowing in.** The first charges are blanks, coke only (the "Coke only" button). With blast on, the furnace blows in; this takes about 40 minutes.
- **Running.** Blast under 30 % for more than an hour of physical time puts the furnace on **banked** standby.
- **Chilled hearth.** No blast or no cooling for half an hour of physical time, about 25 seconds, and the hearth freezes. Recovery is long, on reduced blast.
- **Hearth burnthrough.** Once the hearth lining is worn through, metal and slag break out.

## Casting

The [cast house machine](item:promptcritical:cast_house_machine) by the taphole: the drill opens it, the mud gun plugs it with [taphole clay](item:promptcritical:taphole_clay), 2 units a time. Both act on a right-click or on a rising redstone edge. Hot metal flows along the [iron runner](item:promptcritical:iron_runner), level or one block down per step, into a 2×2×2 [ladle](item:promptcritical:ladle) of 30 t, or onto sand as [pig iron](item:promptcritical:pig_iron). With neither ladle nor sand, iron spreads over the cast house floor. Slag is quenched with water in a [slag granulator](item:promptcritical:slag_granulator) by the runner.

When the hearth is drained, the taphole blows gas: plug it. A missed cast lets the liquid rise to the tuyeres; a tuyere burns through and leaks water into the hearth. Replace a burnt tuyere with a [spare](item:promptcritical:tuyere_spare) by right-clicking it.

A ladle cools on its own. Below the solidus the iron freezes into a skull, knocked out with Shift + right-click at the cost of lining wear. A cold ladle chills the first metal it takes; keep ladles hot by taking cast after cast.
