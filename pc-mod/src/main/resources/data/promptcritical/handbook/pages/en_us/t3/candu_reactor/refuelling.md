## On-power refuelling

Natural uranium carries little excess reactivity, not enough for a long cycle. So a CANDU is refuelled every day, without shutting down.

CANDU bundles are assembled on the assembly jig like any assembly, from natural pellets. Put them in the desk slot: one by one they go into the fuelling machine magazine, up to 24.

The "Refuel" button:

- **on a shut-down reactor** fills the next empty channel with 12 bundles — that is the first core load;
- **at power** pushes 8 fresh bundles into the most burnt channel, and 8 burnt ones come out the far end as one canister in the spent fuel slot. Reactivity rises afterwards.

Simplification: what comes out has the channel's average composition. In a real reactor the most burnt bundles at the outlet end leave.

The scram trips on a period shorter than 10 s, power above 110 %, flow below 70 % at power, the button or an external signal.
