The boiler has two hardware protections. Both work without electricity, without an operator and without software. One protects against excess pressure, the other against low water.

## The safety valve

The valve is built into the boiler. A spring holds the valve disc on its seat. When the steam force on the disc exceeds the spring force, the valve lifts and blows steam to atmosphere.

The set point is 1.1 times working pressure, 1.32 MPa. The valve opens with a whistle and a cloud of steam and reseats when pressure falls. Every lift is steam, and so coal, thrown into the sky.

The whistle of the valve is not an accident but a signal. It means the furnace makes more steam than the engine takes. Cut the coal feed or give the engine load. A safety valve is never tied down or overloaded: it is the only thing between the boiler and a burst shell.

The boiler shell is built with a margin: it bursts at four times working pressure. That margin is there for the valve, not instead of it.

## The fusible plug

A [fusible plug](item:promptcritical:fusible_plug) is a brass plug with a tin core, screwed into the crown of the furnace tube. Tin melts at 232 °C.

While the crown is under water, boiling water at about 190 °C keeps the plug cool and the tin solid. If the level falls below the crown, the metal stops being cooled, the core melts out, and a jet of steam blows into the furnace. The steam puts out the fire and screams: the boiler house knows something is wrong.

Without a plug, low water ends differently. The furnace crown overheats, loses strength and is crushed by pressure. The boiler explodes. Water in a boiler at working pressure is superheated relative to the atmosphere. When the shell fails it flashes to steam at once, and 10 t of water releases energy on the order of tens of kilograms of TNT. This is the main accident of the first epoch, and the cheapest part of the boiler protects against it.

## If the plug has melted

- The fire is already out. Do not add coal and do not relight.
- Find the cause: an empty feed tank, a broken water supply.
- Wait until pressure has fallen almost to zero. A plug is only fitted to a boiler without pressure; under pressure the boiler will not accept it.
- Fit a new plug with a right-click on the boiler. An empty-hand click on the boiler shows whether the plug is intact.
- Refill the tank and light up again, as the first time.

Plugs are made from copper and cassiterite, two at a time. Keep a spare by the boiler.
