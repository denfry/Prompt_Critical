## Power by flow

Steam in the core displaces the moderating water, so the void coefficient is strongly negative: every percent of steam by volume is several hundred pcm. At full power about a third of the core is steam, and the exit quality is 13 %.

That makes **recirculation flow** the main BWR control. Faster pumps sweep the steam out sooner, voids shrink and power rises. Slower pumps and power falls, no rods touched. At 70 % flow the reactor settles at about 73 % power on its own.

Change flow gently, about 0.5 % a second. A jump in pump speed is a jump in reactivity: short period and a scram.

Losing the pumps is not a scram. The reactor drops to natural circulation, about 30 % flow, and lowers its own power.

The scram trips on:

- period shorter than 10 s;
- power above 118 %;
- pressure above 7.6 MPa;
- level below 30 %;
- the button or an external signal.

The vessel safety valve opens at 7.9 MPa.
