## The sodium-water reaction

The main hazard of a sodium plant is a steam generator tube. The water in it is under pressure, and through a crack it flows into the secondary sodium. Sodium and water give caustic NaOH, hydrogen and a lot of heat.

Shift + right-click a BOR-60 steam generator to start the **tube leak drill**: water flows into the sodium at 0.5 kg/s. Shift + right-click again ends the drill: the tube is plugged. The same leak starts by itself if the hot side of the secondary loop runs above 650 °C.

What happens next:

1. Hydrogen builds up in the secondary cover gas, and the sodium-water scram fires at once.
2. The protection cannot be cocked while the leak lasts.
3. When the hydrogen reaches 1 MPa, the rupture disc on the steam generator relief line bursts. There is a bang at the generator, a caustic cloud and a burning hydrogen flame, with damage around it. Players nearby get a message.
4. While the reaction goes on, white caustic fog hangs at the generator nozzles.

On the board the "Na leak" lamp lights and the IHX summary turns red.
