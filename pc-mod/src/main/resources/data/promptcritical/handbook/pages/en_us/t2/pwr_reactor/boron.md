## Boron

A fresh core carries reactivity for the whole cycle, more than rods can hold. Boric acid in the primary water holds it instead: with fresh 4.4 % fuel at power that is about 8 g/kg H₃BO₃.

Each gram per kilogram is about −1.5 % reactivity, or −8.7 pcm per ppm of boron.

The concentration changes by water exchange. "−" and "+" set the flow in 0.5 kg/s steps, up to 5 kg/s, with the same flow to letdown. "Source" picks what goes in:

- **clean condensate** dilutes: boron leaves and reactivity rises;
- **40 g/kg concentrate** borates: reactivity falls.

Dilution is exponential, dC/dt = −(ṁ/M)·C. At 5 kg/s and 7 t in the loop that is about 1 ppm a second, +9 pcm/s. Slow but relentless: a forgotten exchange will run the reactor away.

Xenon after a shutdown eats into the margin too. To restart in the iodine pit, dilute.
