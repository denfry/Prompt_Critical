## VVER-70: Rheinsberg at full size

The first Soviet pressurised water unit built abroad: 265 MW thermal, 132 VVER-440 cassettes, three loops. Everything here is one metre per block: a vessel 19 blocks tall, vertical steam generators, canned main coolant pumps and a gas volume compensator. Drawings and construction work as for the other real-scale machines: a site, stages and metal by weight.

**What the vessel needs nearby:**

- the **gas volume compensator**: a nitrogen cushion holds 10 MPa, there are no heaters. Without the compensator the scram cannot be cocked;
- three **main coolant pumps** on Create shafts, one per loop. Their breaker keys are on the unit board: a pump runs only while its breaker is closed;
- **steam generators** at boiler drums, as for the larger VVER.

The fuel is VVER-440 cassettes; the vessel refuses other assemblies. Put fresh cassettes into the shut-down reactor by hand or with a hopper, or let the refuelling machine bring them from the pool.

**Start-up from the unit board (BPU):**

1. Close the three pump keys and wait for full speed.
2. Ten seconds after the last scram cause has cleared, press "Cock". The scram bank lifts by itself.
3. Withdraw the control group with ▲ in short steps and watch the period dial. A period under 10 s scrams the reactor, so at 20 s already stop the group (■) or insert it (▼).
4. Above 10 % power press "Permit"; without it the scram fires at 25 %.
5. Hold the power with the control group; the boron concentration is in the bottom window.

The ARK indicators show the scram bank (groups 1–5) and the control group (6). The warning protection lamps: PZ-1 means the group is stepping down, PZ-2 means withdrawal is blocked.

**The AZ key** sits under a red-and-white guard: lift it and hold the button for 1.5 s until the bar runs full. Let go earlier and the scram does not fire. The keyboard cannot press it.
