## Purpose

This procedure sets out how to start, run and shut down a fire-tube boiler with its steam engine. Boiler: 5 MW thermal, 1.2 MPa working pressure, safety valve set at 1.32 MPa, 4 m³ feed tank.

## Before start-up

- The boiler is complete, and the flue over the rear top edge is led into the chimney. Chimney height at least 15 m, preferably 20 m.
- The steam engine stands right against the boiler, a Create shaft is connected to its coupling, and a gear is selected.
- The engine's stop valve is closed.
- The fusible plug is intact. Check with an empty-hand click on the boiler.
- The feed tank is full. A water supply is piped in, or buckets are at hand.
- A pressure gauge hangs on the boiler, needle at zero.
- No coal on the grate; slag cleared.

## Start-up

- Wait while the feed regulator fills the boiler to the low level, about 3.5 minutes. Water in the glass is above the red mark.
- Charge one coal and light the furnace with flint and steel, a torch or a fire charge.
- Fire with a thin bed: add one coal at a time while the smoke stays light. On black smoke, stop firing until it clears.
- As the chimney warms, bring the feed up to rated: three coal on the grate or 0.3 kg/s from the stoker.
- At no less than 0.5 MPa on the gauge, open the stop valve.
- If the engine stops on dead centre, bar it over: sneak and right-click with an empty hand.

## Running

- Hold pressure with the coal feed, not with the safety valve. A valve lift is a signal to cut the feed.
- Water must not fall to the red mark on the glass.
- The feed tank must not run dry.
- Clear slag before it buries the grate.
- Smoke light. Black smoke means too little air: cut the feed.
- Engine speed hunting: fit a governor dashpot.

## Shutdown

- Stop the coal feed and let the bed burn out.
- Close the stop valve when the engine is no longer needed.
- Keep feeding the boiler until the pressure has fallen.

## Emergencies

- **Plug melted.** Do not relight, do not charge coal. Remove the cause of low water, wait until pressure is almost zero, fit a new plug, refill the tank. Light up from scratch.
- **Water below the mark, plug intact.** Stop the coal feed at once. Check the tank and the water supply.
- **Valve will not reseat, pressure rising.** Stop the coal feed, open the stop valve and put load on the engine.
- **Create load lost or the network broken.** Close the stop valve and cut the coal feed: the steam has nowhere to go, and pressure will head for the valve setting.
- A boiler that has lost its water with a red-hot furnace crown explodes. Do not stand by a boiler whose water level you do not know.
