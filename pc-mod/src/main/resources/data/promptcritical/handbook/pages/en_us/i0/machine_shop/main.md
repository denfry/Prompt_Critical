A casting from the mould is rough. Bearing seats, flanges and bolt holes are finished by cutting. The I0 machine shop is two machine tools on a Create shaft and the cutting tools for them.

## Machine tools

- The [lathe](item:promptcritical:machine_lathe) turns: the workpiece spins and the tool takes a chip off the diameter. The Create shaft comes in from the left.
- The [drill press](item:promptcritical:drill_press) drills holes. The shaft comes in from behind. Inside is a 1:4 step-up gear, because a drill needs more speed than Create gives.

Each operation is done once: the lathe will not take a casting that has been turned, nor the drill press one that has been drilled. A machine will not take chilled iron at all.

## How to work

- Load a casting with a right-click or feed it with a hopper.
- A [cutting tool](item:promptcritical:cutting_tool) goes into the machine's tool slot. Its wear is the item's durability.
- An empty-hand click on the machine collects finished parts and shows progress, spindle speed and tool wear.

## Cutting power

Cutting takes power, and the machine draws it from the Create network as stress. Power is the product of specific cutting force, depth of cut, feed and speed. Specific cutting force is about 2000 N/mm² for steel, 1100 for cast iron and 700 for aluminium. Cast iron, and aluminium even more so, cut more easily than steel.

## Tool life

A tool wears. How fast is set by cutting speed. Taylor found the relationship: speed multiplied by tool life to the power n is constant. For high-speed steel n is about 0.125.

That means tool life falls as the eighth power of speed. Double the speed and the tool lasts 256 times less. Raise it by 10 % and it lasts half as long.

Hence the machinist's rule: do not drive the machine hard. A fast spindle saves minutes per part and eats tools. Pick a Create gear that keeps the spindle at moderate speed, and change the tool before it is completely blunt.

## Scrap

Machining does not cure a casting defect. A cavity or porosity stays inside the part, and a body with a cavity will leak under pressure. There is no way to see inside a casting in the first epoch, so scrap is prevented in the foundry: a riser of the right size, dry sand, well-superheated metal.
