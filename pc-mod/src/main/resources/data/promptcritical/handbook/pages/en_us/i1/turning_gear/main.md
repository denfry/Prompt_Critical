# Turning gear

A turbine rotor that has just stopped is hot, and it cools unevenly: the top stays warmer than the bottom, and the rotor bends. Left standing hot, it is fully bowed in about half an hour of plant time. Start it like that and it shakes: the imbalance in the vibration model grows up to elevenfold, the vibration transmitter and the analyser show it until the rotor straightens on the run.

The [turning gear](item:promptcritical:turbine_turning_gear) stands against the turbine. Give it a Create shaft of 16 rpm or more on another face, and its reduction gear turns the stopped rotor at 3 rpm: the rotor stays straight and a bow already there goes away. Right-click it for the state of the rotor. A safety device driven by Create kinetics: operators know it, most mods do not.
