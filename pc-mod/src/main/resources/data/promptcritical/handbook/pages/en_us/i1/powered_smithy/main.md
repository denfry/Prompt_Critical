A hand hammer gives sixty joules a blow. A power hammer on a Create shaft gives thousands, so a bloom that takes a smith all evening is drawn out in minutes. The work does not change, only its scale: the piece still has to be hot, and the machine still cannot fix a crack.

## The heating furnace

The [heating furnace](item:promptcritical:heating_furnace) is a brick chamber with a coal burner. Set the temperature with the buttons and the burner holds it, up to 50 kW. A cold chamber takes ten to fifteen minutes to reach forging heat, so light it before you need it. The piece in the chamber heats to the chamber temperature and scales just as it does in a hearth. A wire coil in the chamber anneals once it is above the recrystallisation temperature.

## The power hammer and the crank press

The [power hammer](item:promptcritical:power_hammer) and the [crank press](item:promptcritical:crank_press) take their power from the shaft at the back. The faster the shaft turns, the more energy each stroke carries: the panel shows the shaft power and the stroke energy. Pick drawing out or upsetting, set the target thickness, and press Start. The machine forges by itself while the piece is in the forging range and has not reached the target, then stops. It never strikes a piece that has cooled below the range: take it back to the furnace.

The press strokes four times slower than the hammer, but the metal flows more easily under slow pressure, so the same energy squeezes it more. Upsetting a long bar still buckles it, and the machine stops after anything other than a good series.

## The hardening furnace

The [hardening furnace](item:promptcritical:hardening_furnace) is a heating furnace with a quench tank. Fill the tank with water or quench oil, with a bucket or Create pipes; it needs at least a bucket. Heat the piece above the hardening temperature and press Quench. Then lower the setpoint to the tempering temperature and leave the piece in the chamber: below 650 °C the furnace tempers instead of heating for hardening.

## The wire drawing mill

The [wire drawing mill](item:promptcritical:wire_mill) does the draw plate's work on a shaft. Choose the final diameter and start it: the mill goes from die to die with soap in the box. It stops at size, when the shaft cannot pull the wire through, or before a pass that would snap the hardened wire. Anneal the coil in the furnace and start it again.

Each machine also comes at full size, built on a construction site from its drawing and working the same way. The [pneumatic power hammer](item:promptcritical:power_hammer_real), the [inclinable crank press](item:promptcritical:crank_press_real) and the [wire drawing block](item:promptcritical:wire_mill_real) take the Create shaft at the back cell, on the same side as the small machines, and take the work at the cell in front of it. The [coal-fired reheating furnace](item:promptcritical:heating_furnace_real) and the [muffle hardening furnace](item:promptcritical:hardening_furnace_real) take work through the door at the front; the hardening furnace's quench tank is filled from above.
