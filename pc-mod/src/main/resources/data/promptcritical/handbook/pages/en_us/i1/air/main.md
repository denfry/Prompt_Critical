A boiler stack puts out sulfur dioxide, nitrogen oxides, ash and CO₂. The first three do not vanish: they settle on the chunks around it.

## The plume

Smoke rises above the stack the higher the hotter and faster it leaves (Briggs plume rise), then spreads downwind as a cone (Gaussian plume). Ground-level concentration peaks where the cone meets the ground. Distances in the game are compressed twenty times, so for a 30 m stack the peak sits around the sixth chunk downwind. A taller stack dilutes the smoke; it does not remove it.

## What builds up

Sulfur, nitrogen and dust settle on leaves and soil, and rain washes them out of the air faster. A chunk cleans itself: about two thirds of the load goes into the soil within a day, rain washes it off, and trees take it up. Wind slowly carries it further. The acid load index Φ shows when plants are already suffering.

| Level | When | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Haze | PM₁₀ ≥ 45 µg/m³ | message |
| Smog | PM₁₀ ≥ 150 or SO₂ ≥ 350 | message |
| Plants suffer | Φ ≥ 1 or daily SO₂ ≥ 30 | message |
| Cough | SO₂ ≥ 500 or PM₁₀ ≥ 150 | slowness without a respirator or gas mask |
| Weakness | SO₂ ≥ 2620 (1 ppm) or PM₁₀ ≥ 500 | weakness and slowness |
| Acid rain | rain at pH ≤ 4.5 | message |

The thresholds follow WHO and UNECE guidance. 100 µg/m³ of SO₂ makes rain of about pH 4.2.

## Cleaning

A cleaner works if it stands within 8 blocks of the stack or furnace.

- The [electrostatic precipitator](item:promptcritical:electrostatic_precipitator) catches ash by Deutsch–Anderson: 5000 m² of plates on 100 m³/s of gas give 99.3 %. It needs grid power. Without it the boiler keeps running and keeps dusting.
- The [scrubber](item:promptcritical:fgd_absorber) sprays limestone slurry through the gas and takes about 97 % of the SO₂, using 1.6 kg of limestone (calcite) per kilogram caught.
- A [CEMS analyzer](item:promptcritical:cems_analyzer) at the stack top measures the emissions. Without one, reporting uses the model value plus a 20 % margin.
