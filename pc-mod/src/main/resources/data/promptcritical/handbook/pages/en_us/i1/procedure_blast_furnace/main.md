## Purpose

This procedure sets out how to blow in, run, cast and stop a blast furnace. Furnace: 5×5 shell ring 12–18 blocks high, 4–12 tuyeres, one taphole, hot blast at 900 °C, ore load 3–3.5.

## Before blowing in

- The furnace is complete: the panel shows volume and tuyere count, not a fault message.
- Blowers on Create shafts, stoves and a dust catcher are within 12 blocks of the furnace. At least two stoves, three is better.
- The stoves are piped for fuel gas. For the first blow-in, coke-oven gas.
- The dust catcher is piped to the users of blast-furnace gas.
- Drill and mud gun stand at the taphole; the gun holds taphole clay, 2 per cast.
- The runner leads from the taphole to a ladle or a sand bed. The ladle is in place. The slag granulator by the runner is filled with water.
- Quenched coke, sinter and flux in stock; two spare tuyeres.
- Cooling is on at the panel.

## Blowing in

- Switch on "Coke only": the first charges are coke alone.
- Load coke into the furnace top.
- Heat the stoves "on gas" up to the hot blast setpoint. Switch on the auto cycle.
- Start the blowers. The furnace begins blowing in; this takes about 40 minutes.
- After blowing in, switch "Coke only" off. Raise the ore load in steps of 0.25 up to the working value.

## Running

- Cast every 1.5–2.5 minutes. The drill opens the taphole; when the taphole blows gas, the hearth is drained: plug it with the mud gun.
- Hot metal in the hearth must not rise to the tuyeres. A missed cast means a burnt tuyere.
- Raceway temperature: 2000–2200 °C. Above, lower the hot blast setpoint. Below, add coke (lower the ore load).
- Keep the hanging risk on the panel under one half.
- Coke into the furnace: quenched, M25 80 or better.
- Change ladles before they are full. A full ladle goes to the converter or is cast into pigs.
- Never switch cooling off.

## Stopping

- For a short stop, **bank** the furnace. Bring the blast down to 10–25 % of rated, but not to zero. Within a minute the furnace goes to banked standby, with small heat losses. After that the blast may be taken off.
- A running furnace with no blast chills in 25 seconds. Do not stop the blowers on a running furnace.
- Cast the hearth before stopping.
- To restart from banking: blast at 30 % or more.

## Emergencies

- **Tuyere burnt through.** Water runs into the hearth. Replace the tuyere with a spare at once (right-click), and cast the hearth.
- **Hanging.** Lower the hot blast setpoint and the driving rate, check coke strength. Expect a slip: a gas surge through the bleeders.
- **Chilled hearth.** Restore blast and cooling. The furnace comes back through a new blow-in, on a quarter of the blast, and slowly.
- **Taphole will not open.** The hearth is cold or the taphole is already open: check the panel.
- **Iron spreading over the cast house floor.** No ladle or sand at the runner. Plug the taphole at once.
- **Hearth wear close to 100 %.** A hearth burnthrough throws out metal and slag with explosive force. Keep clear of the taphole and the hearth.
