## Nickel and cobalt

Pentlandite from the copper-nickel seams goes through the same flotation cell and smelter as copper and comes out as nickel matte with 45 % Ni. The matte is cast straight into anodes; the converter does not take it. Switch an empty [electrorefining section](item:promptcritical:electrorefining_cell) to nickel mode. Sulphide anodes need 3.0–3.5 V. 90 % of the nickel plates out as cathodes.

Cobalt follows nickel at 3.5 % of it. It dissolves into the electrolyte, is cleaned out by solvent extraction and electrowon: 50–65 % of it comes out as 1 kg cobalt ingots of 95 % purity, the coarse grade that is good enough for Co-60 targets.

The platinum metals of the ore stay in the anode slime, 50 g in each bag.
