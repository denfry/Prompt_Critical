The first epoch of the complex stands on two things: coal and steam. Coal gives heat, the boiler turns heat into steam, and the steam engine turns steam into rotation of a Create shaft. Everything else in I0, the mine, coal preparation, the foundry and the machine shop, either produces coal or is driven by that shaft.

## Step 1. Find coal

Coal lies in **seams**: flat bodies 1–4 blocks thick and up to a hundred and fifty blocks long, most often under plains, taiga and swamps. Seams come in series, one below another. Under a seam there is usually [fireclay](item:promptcritical:fireclay); it is fired into refractory brick for furnaces. Pyrite occurs nearby.

A seam drops [run-of-mine coal](item:promptcritical:raw_coal). Its rank depends on depth: the deeper, the fewer volatiles and the higher the heating value. See [Coal: ranks and assay](promptcritical:i0/coal). Ordinary Minecraft coal burns in the boiler too.

Rather than searching blind, find a [geological map](item:promptcritical:geological_map). It turns up in chests in abandoned mineshafts, village cartographers' houses, shipwreck map chests and dungeons. Right-click to unfold it: the map marks the nearest ore body or coal seam and gives the distance, bearing and elevation of its centre.

## Step 2. The boiler house and first steam

A boiler, a chimney and a steam engine give you your first mechanical drive. The one thing to understand before lighting up: a cold chimney draws poorly, so a boiler is fired with a thin bed of coal. The order of work is in [Raising steam: the first start](promptcritical:i0/first_steam).

## Step 3. The mine

A seam at the surface runs out quickly. Real production is underground, and it has its own hazards: methane, carbon monoxide, roof falls and water. Read the chapters on [ventilation](promptcritical:i0/mine_ventilation), [explosions](promptcritical:i0/mine_explosions), [roof support](promptcritical:i0/mine_support) and [hoisting and drainage](promptcritical:i0/hoist_drainage).

## Step 4. Coal preparation

Run-of-mine coal carries rock and ash. The [coal jig](promptcritical:i0/coal_prep) splits it into clean coal, middlings and refuse.

## Step 5. Casting and machining

Pumps, valves and flywheels need bodies. They are cast in the [foundry](promptcritical:i0/foundry) and finished in the [machine shop](promptcritical:i0/machine_shop).

## What next

The next epoch is steel and electricity. It needs coke, and coke is made from coking coal, which lies deeper than steam coal. The mine you build in I0 will keep working.
