# Water intake

A [water intake](item:promptcritical:water_intake) stands at the edge of a body of water and gives raw water to the pipes, a litre per millibucket: for water treatment, fire water, cooling.

How much it may take depends on the water. The sea — a biome of ocean or more than 4096 blocks of connected water — has no limit but the screen. A river gives half of its flow at most: the cross-section at the intake times 0.5 m/s. A pond has no current; the intake takes no more than a tenth of its volume a day.

The screen keeps out leaves, fish and mud, and clogs while water runs through it: the clogged share approaches one as 1 − e^(−t/θ), with θ ten days of plant time at 25 °C and twice faster for every ten degrees warmer. A clogged screen passes a tenth of its flow, and the pumps behind it starve. Sneak and right-click to rake it clean.
