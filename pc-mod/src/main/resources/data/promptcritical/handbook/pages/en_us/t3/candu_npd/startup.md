## Starting up on the moderator level

A new reactor is dry and its calandria is empty. Load the core first, while the moderator is still in the tank. The "Fuel" button fills the next empty channel with nine bundles from the bay. 132 channels take 1188 bundles, about 16 t of uranium.

Fill the heat transport circuit to the top through the headers: a half-empty circuit is a voided one. Then pipe some 100 t of heavy water into the dump tank. Start the heat transport pumps and press "Reset" to close the dump valves.

Now raise the moderator with ▲: the pumps lift it about 4 t a minute. The board's tall glass shows the level and how fast it rises. A fresh core goes critical with the calandria only about a third full, so watch the period. Stop with ■ when power starts to climb, and lower the level with ▼ if it climbs too fast.

When you are near the power you want, press "Auto". The controller holds the setpoint you set with − and +. It moves the level so the period stays longer than 30 s, so going from 10 % to full power takes a few minutes. As the fuel burns the level creeps up, and when it reaches the top it is time to refuel.

## The dump

The dump trips on a period shorter than 10 s, power above 110 %, flow below 70 % at power, an external signal, or the guarded "DUMP" button. The helium balance valves open, and the moderator falls through the dump ports into the tank under its own weight. The level drops by a third in about three seconds, and the reactor is shut down.

Coming back is slow. "Reset" closes the valves only once the cause is gone. Then the pumps have to lift all the heavy water again, which takes over twenty minutes. Xenon builds up in the meantime and can keep the reactor down for hours. So do not dump for nothing.
