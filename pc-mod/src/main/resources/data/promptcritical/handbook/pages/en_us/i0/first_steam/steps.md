## Raising steam step by step

- **Step 1.** Place the boiler. It goes down whole, 3×3×6, empty and cold. Leave room at the side for the engine and at the front for the stoker and gauge.
- **Step 2.** Build the chimney: sections one on top of another over the rear top edge of the boiler, above the flue. Aim for 20 m, about 17 sections. A short chimney means weak draught and a long warm-up.
- **Step 3.** Place the steam engine right against the boiler. It couples to the boiler whose block it touches. Bring a Create shaft to the engine's shaft coupling. A Create wrench on the coupling changes the gear: 16, 32 or 64 rpm at rated engine speed.
- **Step 4.** Fill with water. A bucket or a Create pipe into any block of the boiler fills its 4 m³ feed tank. The feed regulator then fills the boiler itself, in about 3.5 minutes. While pressure is below half of working pressure, it holds the **low** level, 0.10 m above the crown of the furnace tubes: water swells as it heats, which is how real boilers are lit. Keep the tank from running dry.
- **Step 5.** Coal feed. By hand, right-click the boiler with coal: one item is 100 kg, with no more than 500 kg on the grate. Or place a chain grate stoker at the front and bring a Create shaft in from the left: 0.3 kg/s at 32 rpm, feed proportional to speed.
- **Step 6.** Throw in **one** coal and light it: flint and steel, a torch or a fire charge on the boiler, once there is coal on the grate.
- **Step 7.** Watch the smoke. Light smoke: add another coal. Black smoke: wait until it clears. As the chimney warms, bring the feed up to rated: three coal on the grate or 0.3 kg/s.
- **Step 8.** Hang the pressure gauge on the boiler front. An empty-hand click on the boiler shows the bed on the grate, the water in the tank, the furnace state and the plug state.
- **Step 9.** Wait for at least half of working pressure, about 0.5 MPa on the gauge. The engine's stop valve is closed by default. Open it with an empty-hand right-click on the engine. At low pressure an engine under Create load creeps onto dead centre and stops.
- **Step 10.** Stuck on dead centre? Sneak and right-click the engine with an empty hand to bar it over. The crank turns 60°. This works only on an engine that has nearly stopped.
- **Step 11.** The engine is up to speed: "First Steam" is yours. Fit a [governor dashpot](item:promptcritical:governor_dashpot) to the engine so the speed stops hunting.
- **Step 12.** Clear the slag from time to time with an empty hand or a hopper: [boiler slag](item:promptcritical:boiler_slag), 10 kg of ash per item.

## Common mistakes

- Grate loaded all at once: black smoke and half an hour of waiting.
- Stop valve opened too early: the engine stopped on dead centre.
- Feed tank ran dry: the regulator has nothing to feed the boiler with. That is the direct road to low water.
- The engine takes less steam than the furnace makes: pressure rises to the safety valve setting. A whistle and a cloud of steam mean cut the coal feed.
