A mine begins when you take out the first block of a coal seam. The workings are the connected air under the surface. The mod tracks it: how much methane, carbon monoxide and oxygen it holds and where it moves.

## Where methane comes from

Coal holds methane in its pores. Every exposed block of seam gives off gas: strongly at first, then less and less, over a couple of days. Broken coal gives off the rest within an hour. The deeper the seam, the more gas it holds.

Methane is lighter than air and collects **under the roof**. Where air stands still or moves slower than 0.5 m/s, there is twice as much gas at the roof as the average for the heading. Dead ends, high spots and the cavities of roof falls are the first places it gathers.

## Ventilation

Gas is not removed; it is diluted and carried out by fresh air. That is the job of the [main mine fan](item:promptcritical:main_mine_fan): 3×3×3, with a Create shaft brought in from behind. It is set in the mouth of a heading so that one side opens to the outside and the other into the mine. It draws air out of the mine, and fresh air enters through the other openings.

Air takes the path of least resistance. To make it sweep the face rather than short-cut straight to the fan, fit [ventilation doors](item:promptcritical:ventilation_door). A closed door is almost a wall to the air, with a few per cent leakage. An open door short-circuits the air current and leaves the face without air. Open doors with a right-click and close them behind you at once.

Fan flow is proportional to speed, pressure to speed squared. When the fan stops, gas starts to build. In a large mine 1 % methane gathers in minutes, in a dead-end heading in tens of seconds. Ventilation is a duty that never stops.

## Limits

- Return air from a face: no more than 1 % methane.
- Mine return: 0.75 %; intake: 0.5 %.
- Local accumulation: no more than 2 %.
- Air velocity: at least 0.25 m/s everywhere and no more than 4 m/s on a longwall face.

## How to measure

- A [canary](item:promptcritical:canary) in a [canary cage](item:promptcritical:canary_cage). It senses carbon monoxide and lack of oxygen, not methane. A bird takes up CO about 20 times faster than a man: it grows restless, then falls off its perch. If it falls, leave. Take a fallen but living bird with an empty hand and carry it into fresh air, and it will come round.
- A [flame safety lamp](item:promptcritical:flame_safety_lamp) in hand. From 1 % methane a blue cap rises over the lowered flame and grows towards 5 %. Above 5 % the mixture burns inside the gauze: get out at once. Below 17 % oxygen the lamp goes out.
- A [methanometer](item:promptcritical:methanometer): right-click to measure methane at the roof. From 1 % the reading turns red.
- A [mine gas monitoring cabinet](item:promptcritical:mine_dispatch_cabinet) raises the alarm when methane anywhere in the mine reaches 1 %. An empty-hand click gives a summary: air intake, gas peaks, drainage.

A [self-rescuer](item:promptcritical:self_rescuer): right-click, and for 30 minutes you breathe from the set, not from the heading. It is single-use.

## Degasification

Part of the methane can be drawn from the seam before it reaches the workings. A [degasification borehole](item:promptcritical:degasification_borehole) with a vacuum pump on a Create shaft next to it takes gas from the strata and cuts the methane released into the face.
