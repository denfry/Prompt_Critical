# Personal protective equipment

Protective equipment changes what the plant does to you, not what it does to the plant. Everything here is worn armour with its own layer.

The [hard hat](item:promptcritical:hard_hat) cuts the damage of falling blocks and objects to 0.3. It has one insert: click a [gas mask](item:promptcritical:gas_mask) or [ear muffs](item:promptcritical:hearing_protection) onto it in the inventory, right-click it empty-handed to take the insert out. Ear muffs in the hat take 25 dB off the noise dose, as on their own.

A gas mask cleans the air fifty times, but only of the gas its [filter](item:promptcritical:gas_mask_filter) is made for: A for organic vapours, AX for low-boiling organics, B for chlorine and hydrogen sulphide, E for sulphur dioxide and hydrogen fluoride, K for ammonia, CO (hopcalite) for carbon monoxide, P for dust, smoke and radioactive aerosols. The sorbent fills up with what it stops: a class 1 B filter holds 1000 ppm of chlorine for twenty minutes, then the gas breaks through. Click a new filter onto the mask; an unused old one comes back.

A filtering mask adds no oxygen. Below 17 % O₂ it does not protect at all, and nitrogen, argon, carbon dioxide or methane pass any filter. For that there is the [breathing apparatus](item:promptcritical:scba): 2000 L of air, 40 L a minute, about fifty minutes, a whistle at 20 % left. While it has air, nothing around you reaches your lungs. Fill it by clicking an [air cylinder](item:promptcritical:scba_cylinder) onto it.

The [fire suit](item:promptcritical:fire_suit) takes radiant heat to a tenth and burning to a fifth, but slows you, and after five minutes of work you overheat. The [safety harness](item:promptcritical:safety_harness) with a [lifeline](item:promptcritical:lifeline) clipped to a support within 16 m makes a fall harmless; the same lifeline lets an attendant winch a man out of a confined space.
