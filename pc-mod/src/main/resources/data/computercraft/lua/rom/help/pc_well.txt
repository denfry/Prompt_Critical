Wells and the drilling rig.

pc_well (wellhead): chokeUp()  chokeDown()  toggleMasterValve()
pc_well (pumpjack): read its panel with read and readMany; event pc_pump_state when it starts or stops
pc_drilling (driller's console): wobUp()  wobDown()  spmUp()  spmDown()  toggleBop()  advanceKill()  zeroPitTotalizer()

Every method returns a ticket. Watch the pit volume with watch() to catch a kick early.
