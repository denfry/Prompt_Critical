# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, argue about engineering with sources rather than about
people, and keep issues and discussions on topic. Harassment, personal attacks
and discriminatory language are not tolerated anywhere in the project's spaces.

Report problems privately to the maintainer at dabinayo@pm.me. Reports are kept
confidential. The maintainer may edit or remove content and block participants
who break these rules.
